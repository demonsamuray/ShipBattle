# Image and asset rights

Status updated 2026-10-02 from the official asset license and the project owner's written confirmation in this conversation.

## Kenney Pirate Pack

The bundled pirate ships, numbered terrain tiles, effects, and matching source sheets include artwork from Kenney's Pirate Pack. Kenney's official asset page identifies the pack's license as Creative Commons CC0: [Kenney Pirate Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/pirate-pack). Kenney states that assets on its asset pages are public domain under CC0, may be used in commercial projects, and do not require attribution: [Kenney licensing and attribution](https://kenney.nl/support). No attribution is required. Optional credit: “Pirate Pack by Kenney.”

## Project and company artwork

The project owner confirms that all other image and visual assets distributed with this project—including the Jungle Gaming logo, title and menu backgrounds, UI artwork, reference/sample images, and other non-Kenney graphics—are owned by the owner or the owner's company, or are otherwise controlled by them with permission to use, modify, publish, and redistribute them with this project.

This owner confirmation covers the source repository and public game build. No additional third-party image license or attribution requirement is identified as outstanding by the owner. The confirmation is recorded as the rights basis for this release; this workspace audit does not independently verify employment assignments, contracts, or original creation records.

## Audio

The bundled WAV files are unused and outside the current release scope. They are not included in the production build; no audio rights are required for the hosted game in its current form.
